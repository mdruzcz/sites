## What changed and why

The site was rebuilt around three asks: a different logo position, a proper contact form, and a popup for the new **Free AI Home Preview** offer, with the Illumico page rhythm (pill eyebrows, hairline icon tiles, five-step timeline, split image/text feature, gallery teaser, boxed "book" CTA, four-column footer) and a lot more indexable content.

### Header
Two-tier with a **centred logo**. Desktop row 1: phone + hours left, logo centre, "Free AI Preview" + "Book a Free Demo" right; row 2: centred nav with a Services dropdown. Mobile: hamburger left, logo centre, call icon right. The logo file is `public/images/logo-dark.png`, a version of `logo.png` with the dark "LIGHTING" text recoloured white so it reads on the dark bar (generated by recolouring low-luminance, low-saturation pixels with sharp).

### Free AI Home Preview (popup)
`AiPreviewProvider` wraps the app and renders a portal modal. It auto-opens once per visitor per 7 days (`localStorage` key `cl_ai_popup_seen`) after ~11s, 45% scroll, or desktop exit-intent, never on `/ai-preview`, `/contact` or legal pages, and never in the first 3.5s. Any `AiPreviewButton` opens it on demand. The form compresses the photo client-side to 1600px JPEG, then POSTs to `/api/ai-preview`, which verifies Turnstile, stores the photo in the private `light-designs` Storage bucket under `celebratelighting/<code>/`, emails the team with the photo attached + a 60-day signed link, emails the customer a confirmation, and writes a lead row (service `Free AI Home Preview`) through the forms Worker. **The preview itself is produced manually by Matt and emailed within 1 business day; nothing generates imagery automatically.** Storage requires `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY` (added to Vercel prod 2026-10-07); without it the route still emails the attachment.

### Contact form
`/contact` has the full `ContactForm` (service select, message, preferred contact) and reads `?service=` to preselect. Hero/closing sections use `QuoteFormCompact`. Both hit `/api/quote`, which is email-first with the Worker row as backup (502 only if both fail).

### Photos
25 roofline photos were sourced from illumicomichigan.com and lakeshorelights.com (roofline portfolio), filed in `public/images/gallery/` with descriptive SEO filenames, resized to ≤1920px, and captioned **from the actual image**. Shots carrying competitor branding or signage, text overlays, landscape/bistro work and product diagrams were deliberately excluded. `scratchpad photos.js` holds the pick list. No C9 big-bulb photos exist yet; seasonal pages use red/green/white permanent shots, so the seasonal gallery strip still needs real C9 photos.

### Tokens (`app/globals.css`)
Token names are unchanged from the July redesign (`--bg`, `--surface`, `--panel`, `--foreground`, `--muted`, `--accent`, `--gold`, `--border`) so the untouched blog/legal pages still render. Buttons moved from pills to 12px radius; `.pill` is the eyebrow; `.icon-tile` the hairline icon box; `.timeline` the numbered process; `.faq-item` the accordion; `.prose-dark` long-form.
