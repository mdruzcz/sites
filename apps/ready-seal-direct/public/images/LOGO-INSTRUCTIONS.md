# Logo

Save the Holiday Lights Direct logo (red Christmas house + wordmark) at this exact path:

    public/images/logo.png

The header will pick it up automatically. Until you do that, the header
falls back to a styled-text wordmark.

Recommended dimensions: ~640×260 px (the image will be displayed at ~h-12
on the header, ~h-16 on mobile menu). PNG with transparent background.
